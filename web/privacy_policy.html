Privacy First

Privacy Policy for GBA Pocket

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

Summary

GBA Pocket collects zero personal data, includes zero tracking SDKs, displays zero ads, and operates 100% offline.

1. Zero Data Collection

GBA Pocket does not collect, record, process, or transmit any user data or personal identifiers. No account registration, email address, name, or phone number is ever requested.

2. Local Storage & File Access

The application only interacts with your device's local file storage when you explicitly choose to:

  • Select a .gba, .bin, or .agb ROM file to play via your device's file picker.
  • Save and load gameplay states or in-game battery progress (.sav files).

All game files and save data remain entirely on your local device and are never transmitted to external servers.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: Used strictly to provide tactile haptic feedback when pressing virtual buttons.
  • Audio: Used strictly for in-game sound effects and music output.

4. Legal & Emulation Disclaimer

GBA Pocket is an independent emulator developed for educational, archival, and entertainment purposes. GBA Pocket is not affiliated with, endorsed, or authorized by Nintendo Co., Ltd. The application does not contain or provide any copyrighted commercial ROMs or proprietary Nintendo BIOS files.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, please contact the developer via the official support repository.

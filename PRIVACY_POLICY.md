# Privacy Policy for GBA Pocket

**Last updated:** October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **GBA Pocket** mobile application (the "App") developed as a Game Boy Advance emulation and entertainment application.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
**GBA Pocket does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.**
- No account registration is required.
- No analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking SDKs are embedded.
- No third-party advertisements or ad networks are integrated.
- No device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID) are accessed or recorded.

### 2. Device Permissions & File Storage
The App requires access to the device's local file storage solely for the following user-directed functions:
- **Loading ROM files**: Reading `.gba`, `.bin`, or `.agb` backup files selected explicitly by the user via the system file picker.
- **Save States & Battery Saves**: Writing and reading gameplay progress (`.sav` files and save state snapshots) locally to device storage.

All game files and save data remain strictly on your local device. The App never uploads, transmits, or synchronizes your ROMs or save data to external servers.

### 3. Audio & Vibration
- **Audio Output**: Used exclusively for in-game sound effects and background music playback.
- **Haptic Vibration**: Used solely to provide tactile touch feedback when tapping on-screen buttons. The App never records audio or accesses the microphone.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because no personal data is collected, the App complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### 5. Third-Party Services
The App contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect user information. It operates entirely offline without requiring an active internet connection.
